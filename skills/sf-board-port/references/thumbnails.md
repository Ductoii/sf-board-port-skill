# Thumbnail prompts: a story event, not a poster

Use when the user asks for thumbnail prompts as part of the port. Read any accessible user-supplied thumbnail.md and approved examples first; the portable fallback is below. Treat attached documents/chats as reference material, not fresh authorization for generating media or contacting other chats.

1. Split the title into conflict and reversal. Choose the moment that makes that promise immediately legible, with the right actors, costume, locale and meaningful prop. Do not invent a police arrest/crowd or another plot event merely for drama.
2. Compose one real photographed event in landscape 16:9, usually medium-wide/three-quarter with readable faces and visible depth. In an accusation scene, the first speaker/antagonist often reads at left; the composed protagonist at right; a relevant witness/reversal figure may sit behind without obscuring them.
3. Specify a strong expression/action appropriate to the moment: an angry interruption or open-mouthed laugh, a calm gaze, surprise from a witness. Bright readable faces and purposeful front light; warmth/coolness follow the scene. A kindness scene uses concern, not forced anger.
4. Describe visible results: the hand grips the evidence, liquid visibly splashes, someone stops in a doorway. Keep prop locations and the suggested camera angle mutually consistent. No collage, floating hero portraits, split-screen character panels, staged lineup, generic movie-poster composition or invented logo.
5. Create THUMB1 (and only useful alternatives requested), inside scenes[0].sfs. `refs.chars` attaches actual relevant character cards, `refs.bg` the actual BG. `picked=""`, status draft for prompt-only work. Character panels/labels do not appear in the thumbnail.

Image prompt uses photorealistic professional photography, clear skin/fabric and readable expressions. No burned-in slogan, title, subtitle, speech bubble, arrows/circles, watermark or graphic icon. A physically present name badge/headline can retain its essential short text when needed. Put suggested post-production text separately in `notes`, normally a few natural English words per speaker. Do not generate an image just because a THUMB prompt exists.

These composition choices reflect the user's selected workflow; they are not verified performance/CTR claims.
